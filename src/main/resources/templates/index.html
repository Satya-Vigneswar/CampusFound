HOW CAMPUSFIND HELPS

Useful leads, reviewed by students

When a report is posted, CampusFind compares its words with open reports of the opposite type. Up to five candidates scoring at least 15% are suggested.

01

Compare useful details

Title (35%), category (20%), description (25%), and location (20%) each contribute to a simple word-overlap score.

02

Review promising leads

Potential matches are ranked and shown on report details and your dashboard so you can decide what is relevant.

03

You stay in control

A match score is a similarity aid, not a probability or confirmation. CampusFind never marks an item returned automatically.
